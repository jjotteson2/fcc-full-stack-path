Tater Tot Casserole

This is a good meal for the family. It is easy to make. It is a good meal for dinner that everyone will like

Ingredients

  • Tater Tots
  • ground beef
  • cheese
  • sause

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
  2. Put ground beef in casserole dish
  3. Put tater tots on
  4. Put in oven for an hour
  5. Enjoy the tater tot casserole
